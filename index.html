About The Class

Qualified Teacher B.Sc. Physical Science

Teaching Mathematics The Right Way

Hello, I am Ravindu Chamara, a dedicated mathematics teacher with a B.Sc. in Physical Science from the University of Peradeniya. I believe every student has the potential to excel in mathematics when taught with patience and clarity.

My teaching approach focuses on building a strong foundation through conceptual understanding, followed by consistent practice and exam-oriented preparation. I currently teach at Queens College International.
